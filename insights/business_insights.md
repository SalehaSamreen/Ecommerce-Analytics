# E-Commerce Sales & Profitability — Business Insights

## 1. Overall Business Performance

The dataset contains 9,994 sales records covering 2014–2017.

- Total Revenue: $2,297,200.86
- Total Profit: $286,397.02
- Total Orders: 5,009
- Unique Customers: 793
- Overall Profit Margin: 12.47%

The business generated strong overall revenue, but profitability varies significantly across products, categories, regions, and discount levels.

---

## 2. Furniture Has Low Profitability Despite High Revenue

Furniture generated approximately $742K in revenue, making it one of the largest categories by revenue.

However, it generated only $18.45K in profit, resulting in a profit margin of just 2.49%.

Technology generated approximately $836K in revenue and $145.45K in profit, with a 17.40% profit margin.

### Business implication

Furniture requires further investigation into pricing, discounting, product-level margins, and potentially high-cost products.

---

## 3. Higher Discounts Are Associated With Lower Profit Margins

The analysis shows a strong relationship between discount levels and profitability.

- 0% discount → 29.51% profit margin
- 20% discount → 11.82% profit margin
- 30% discount → -10.05% profit margin
- 40% discount → -19.81% profit margin
- 70% discount → -98.66% profit margin
- 80% discount → -180.03% profit margin

### Business implication

Higher discount levels should be reviewed carefully because heavily discounted sales can result in negative profitability.

Discount policies could be evaluated by product category, region, and product to identify where discounts are reducing margins excessively.

> Note: The analysis identifies an association between discount levels and profitability; it does not establish causation.

---

## 4. Regional Profitability Varies

The West region generated the highest total profit:

- West: $108.42K profit, 14.94% margin
- East: $91.52K profit, 13.48% margin
- South: $46.75K profit, 11.93% margin
- Central: $39.71K profit, 7.92% margin

### Business implication

The Central region has a relatively low profit margin despite generating approximately $501K in revenue.

Further investigation into discounting, product mix, and product-level profitability in this region may help identify improvement opportunities.

---

## 5. Revenue Does Not Always Mean Profitability

Several high-revenue products generated very low or negative profits.

For example:

- Cisco TelePresence System EX90: $22.64K revenue but -$1.81K profit
- HON 5400 Series Task Chairs: $21.87K revenue with almost zero profit
- GBC DocuBind P400: $17.97K revenue but -$1.88K profit

### Business implication

Product performance should be evaluated using both revenue and profit rather than revenue alone.

---

## 6. High-Revenue Customers Can Still Be Unprofitable

Sean Miller generated approximately $25.04K in revenue but produced a loss of approximately $1.98K.

### Business implication

Customer value should be evaluated using profitability as well as sales volume.

High-revenue customers with negative profitability may require investigation into discounting, product mix, or order economics.

---

# Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

1. Review high-discount transactions, particularly discounts of 30% or more.
2. Investigate the low profitability of the Furniture category.
3. Analyze Central region transactions to identify the reasons behind its lower margin.
4. Monitor product-level profitability instead of focusing only on revenue.
5. Evaluate high-revenue but low-profit customers and products.
6. Use profitability-based metrics alongside revenue when making pricing and sales decisions.
